1. **Mandatory 4-Step Audit**: Before writing or refactoring major architecture or engine code, evaluate:
   - What's technically sound
   - What's overengineered
   - What can be simplified
   - What's the ideal minimal implementation
2. **No Anthropomorphic Hardcoding**: Do not inject domain-specific template rules where general physical/information-theoretic principles (compression, potential fields, variational inference) can discover them.
3. **Continuous Flow Over Discrete Loops**: Anchor agent attention and persistence in metabolic momentum and information gradients, avoiding arbitrary execution loops.
